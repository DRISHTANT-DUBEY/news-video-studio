# NewsForge UI milestones

M1: Broadcast workspace shell, live monitor, inspector, navigation and read-only timeline. Preserve video upload and ticker preview.

M2: Multi-track timeline, draggable and trimmable clips, frame-accurate seek, undo/redo.

M3: Voice-over upload/recording, waveforms, audio mixing and ducking.

M4: News script, editable captions, SRT, opt-in transcription and AI narration.

M5: MP4 browser/cloud render engines, progress, cancellation and 16:9 / 9:16 social variants.

Before merge: typecheck, build, accessibility checks and manual video-preview smoke test. No export or social publishing until backend is implemented.
